What this is. The median operating margin across every general acute-care hospital that files a Medicare cost report (short-term acute + critical access, ~4,300-4,500 a year after exclusions, every ownership type) — a census to check Kaufman Hall's ~1,300-hospital sample against, once a year. Definition (KH-style, not identical). Operating margin = (operating revenue − total operating expenses) / operating revenue, from Worksheet G-3 of Form CMS-2552-10: operating revenue = net patient revenue (line 3) + other income (line 25) less contributions (6), investment income (7), governmental appropriations (23), federal COVID relief (24.50, plus line-24 entries labelled PRF/CARES/PPP etc.) and line-24 entries whose label says investment income, gains, appropriations, taxes or contributions; expenses = line 4. Most line-24 dollars carry generic labels (“other operating revenue”) and stay in — their size is the “unclassified other revenue” column. Kaufman Hall's operating margin likewise excludes investment income. All USD nominal. Years. A cost report counts in the calendar year its fiscal year ENDS (a June-year-end hospital's FY2024 is Jul-2023 to Jun-2024). The December year-end hospitals (only reports covering exactly 1 Jan - 31 Dec) have FY = calendar year, so that column is the like-for-like window for Kaufman Hall's calendar-year figure (a mean of its monthly medians, with the month count shown). Still not identical: a median of annual margins is not a mean of monthly medians, and Kaufman Hall standardises its sample and treats outliers in undisclosed ways. Cost-report expenses carry a system's allocated home-office costs, so the census is closer in concept to the “incl. allocations” line. Excluded. Hospital-years with operating revenue under $1,000,000 or a cost-reporting period outside 300-400 days (counts per year in the table). No margin-based trim. Psychiatric, rehab, long-term-care and children's hospitals are outside this universe. Government hospitals are shown with and without their tax subsidy: excluding it is the operating view, but for a county hospital the subsidy is the funding model. Check on the definition. The revenue-weighted aggregate for short-term acute hospitals outside Maryland (an IPPS proxy) is printed beside MedPAC's all-payer operating margin (MedPAC March 2025 / March 2026 reports to Congress, as summarised by HFMA (hfma.org); FY2023 revised from 5.1% to 5.2% in the 2026 report); ours runs 0.3-1.3 pp lower. Adding back the label-identified line-24 investment items closes most of the gap (measured 2026-10-20), so MedPAC evidently keeps those in. Payer mix is the share of inpatient DAYS (S-3 Part I; Medicare = fee-for-service + Medicare Advantage), not of revenue. Lag and cadence. Annual cost reports; CMS republishes the files quarterly. A fiscal year is complete roughly 10-22 months after it ends (5-month filing deadline + CMS processing); hollow points are partial years. Latest complete FY: 2025.
