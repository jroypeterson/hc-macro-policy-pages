Tier 1 · Medicare Advantage payment rates · CY2027

CMS set a +2.5% expected MA revenue change for CY2027, 2.4 pp above the Advance Notice.

Why it matters: Final next-year MA payment rates + risk model; sets MA revenue for UNH/HUM/CVS/ELV/CNC/MOH/ALHC/CLOV. The Advance Notice (about February) is CMS's first estimate; the Rate Announcement (early April) is final, and the move between them is what the stocks trade. Next release: Feb 15, 2027 (est.).
