Medicare Advantage — rate-announcement decomposition

MA is the highest-$ managed-care exposure and, unlike the PPS systems, its bottom-line expected average change in revenue is built up from the effective growth rate (the “real” rate, which decks quote) plus a stack of adjustments — rebasing/re-pricing, Star-Ratings quality bonus, the statutory coding-pattern cut, risk-model revision & normalization, and the risk-score trend. Advance Notice is CMS's preliminary estimate (~Feb); the Rate Announcement is the final (~April) — the AN→RA move is the tradable surprise. Every value is sourced (see the table links).
