2027 Medicare Star Ratings · released Oct 8, 2026

2027 Star Ratings are out: 71% of members are in 4+ star plans. The bonus changed hands.

The industry share held at 71%. Underneath, 4.4M members are in plans that rose over 4 stars, the line that pays the quality bonus, and 4.9M are in plans that fell below it.

Companies shown: the 10 largest parent organizations by members, plus every other listed insurer; “All other plans” pools the remaining 296 parent organizations (largest: Highmark 425K, MHH Healthcare 379K, Healthfirst 373K, Aware Integrated 344K, Lifetime Healthcare 334K).

Release cadence: CMS publishes Star Ratings once a year, in early-to-mid October, for the following plan year (2027 ratings released Oct 8, 2026; 2028 ratings expected October 2027); the plan crosswalk follows in early October; enrollment by plan is monthly, mid-month.
