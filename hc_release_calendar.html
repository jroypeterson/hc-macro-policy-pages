🏛️ US Government Healthcare Data-Release Calendar — 2026-10-05

A cadence-organized calendar of US-government healthcare data releases + statutory milestones (CMS/HHS/OACT, MedPAC/MACPAC, BLS/BEA), for anticipating catalysts. Each row carries the issuer, next-expected date, why it moves HC stocks, and a source link. ~projected dates carry a band; monthly next-dates recompute each run.

  • T1 Tier 1 · market-moving — First print of a number that resets revenue or margin for covered names; the covered stocks reprice on the day.
  • T2 Tier 2 · read-through — A measurable input to a covered thesis (share, enrollment, pricing, cost inflation), but incremental, telegraphed or lagged; rarely moves a stock by itself.
  • T3 Tier 3 · background — Slow (multi-month lag), schedule-only, or a research/screening input.
